About Ahmed Dezan Tours

Your trusted partner for unforgettable Zanzibar experiences

What We Offer

We are a local tour operator dedicated to showcasing the authentic beauty of Zanzibar. With years of experience, we ensure every journey is seamless, safe, and memorable.

  • Full food coverage – All meals included, from local delicacies to fresh seafood.
  • Convenient pickups – We pick you up from your hotel or airport, no hassle.
  • Ticket & entry management – We handle all entrance fees, permits, and reservations.
  • Flexible itineraries – Tailor your days to suit your pace and interests.
  • Fluent guides – English, Swahili, and other languages available.
  • Reliable transport – Modern, air‑conditioned vehicles with professional drivers.

We take care of every detail so you can focus on making memories.

Why Travel With Us

We believe in creating genuine connections – with the island, its people, and its culture. Our guides are locals who share their passion and insider knowledge.

  • Small group sizes for personal attention.
  • Commitment to sustainable tourism and community support.
  • 24/7 support during your tour.
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs.
