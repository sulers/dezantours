Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Zanzibar?
Most visitors require a visa. You can apply online or obtain one upon arrival at the airport. We recommend checking with the Tanzanian embassy for the latest requirements.

What is the best time to visit Zanzibar?
The dry seasons (June–October and December–February) offer the best weather. However, Zanzibar is beautiful year-round.

Is the tour suitable for children?
Yes, our tours are family-friendly. We can adjust itineraries to accommodate younger travelers.

What should I pack?
Light clothing, sunscreen, a hat, swimwear, comfortable walking shoes, and a camera. Don't forget insect repellent.

Can I customize my itinerary?
Absolutely! We offer flexible options. Just let us know your preferences and we'll tailor the experience.

Cancellation Policy

Flexible Cancellations
We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair:

  • More than 30 days before departure: Full refund (minus any non-refundable deposits).
  • 15–30 days before departure: 50% refund.
  • Less than 15 days before departure: No refund, but we may offer credit toward a future tour.
  • No-shows: No refund.

For group bookings, cancellation fees apply per person. We recommend travel insurance to cover unforeseen circumstances.

In case of government-imposed travel restrictions, we offer full credit for a later date.
